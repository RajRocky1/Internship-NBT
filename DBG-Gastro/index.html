Common Digestive Concerns

What Digestive Symptoms Are You Experiencing?

Digestive disorders manifest in diverse ways. Explore common concerns below to understand their symptoms, clinical approach, and when to seek specialist evaluation.

Acidity & Acid Reflux (GERD)

Persistent heartburn, sour acid regurgitation into the throat, or a burning sensation behind the breastbone often indicate Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD). Over time, chronic reflux can cause esophageal mucosal irritation and requires targeted medical protocols.

Common Indicators:
  • Frequent post-meal burning
  • Regurgitation of acid or food
  • Discomfort worsened lying down
  • Difficulty or pain swallowing
Specialist Focus

Diagnostic Workup

We evaluate your dietary triggers, medication response, and recommend Upper GI Endoscopy only when symptoms persist or red-flag features are present.

Consult Dr. Deepak

Gastritis & Indigestion (Dyspepsia)

Gastritis involves inflammation or erosion of the stomach's protective lining. Symptoms include gnawing upper abdominal burning, early satiety, nausea, and frequent belching. Identifying factors like H. pylori or medication irritation is essential for lasting relief.

Common Indicators:
  • Upper stomach ache or burning
  • Feeling full after small meals
  • Chronic nausea or loss of appetite
  • Recurrent unrefreshing digestion
Specialist Focus

Mucosal Healing

Structured therapy focusing on mucosal protection, eradicating underlying irritants, and establishing gut-friendly nutrition.

Consult Dr. Deepak

Abdominal Discomfort & Pain

Abdominal pain can stem from numerous digestive organs, ranging from simple gas entrapment to gallbladder gallstones, peptic ulceration, or appendix inflammation. Systematic clinical localization is the key to prompt and safe recovery.

Common Indicators:
  • Localized cramp or sharp ache
  • Pain radiating to back or shoulder
  • Discomfort tied to fatty foods
  • Tenderness on gentle pressure
Specialist Focus

Clinical Diagnostic Map

Systematic abdominal palpation, ultrasound imaging guidance, and targeted evaluation ensure safe diagnosis without unnecessary tests.

Consult Dr. Deepak

Chronic Bloating & Gas

Persistent abdominal distension, tightness, and excessive flatulence can arise from delayed stomach emptying, food intolerances, bacterial imbalances, or functional gut motility disorders.

Common Indicators:
  • Visible abdominal swelling by evening
  • Heavy, uncomfortable sensation
  • Sluggish intestinal transit
  • Discomfort after specific foods
Specialist Focus

Motility Optimization

Personalised dietary adjustments, microbiome support, and gut motility optimization to restore light and effortless digestion.

Consult Dr. Deepak

IBS & Bowel Irregularities

Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) is a common gastrointestinal disorder affecting how the brain and gut communicate. It typically presents as alternating diarrhea and constipation, abdominal cramps, and relief after bowel evacuation.

Common Indicators:
  • Unpredictable stool frequency
  • Straining or urgent bowel calls
  • Cramping relieved by passing stool
  • Mucus in stool without bleeding
Specialist Focus

Gut-Brain Axis Protocols

Individualized treatment incorporating gentle motility regulators, stress-gut counseling, and targeted dietary strategies.

Consult Dr. Deepak

Chronic Constipation

Infrequent bowel movements (fewer than three per week), hard lumpy stools, and excessive straining can signal slow colonic transit or pelvic floor dyssynergia. Addressing causes early prevents secondary anorectal complications.

Common Indicators:
  • Straining during bowel movement
  • Sensation of incomplete evacuation
  • Hard or dry stool consistency
  • Reliance on over-the-counter laxatives
Specialist Focus

Colonic Regularity

Structured fibre titrations, osmotic motility support, and specialized evaluation to restore natural, strain-free bowel habits.

Consult Dr. Deepak

Liver & Biliary Health

The liver is your primary metabolic organ. Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD), elevated liver enzymes (SGOT/SGPT), and gallbladder sluggishness frequently develop silently without pronounced early pain. Early screening preserves long-term cellular health.

Common Indicators:
  • Unexplained chronic fatigue
  • Incidental fatty liver on ultrasound
  • Heaviness in upper right abdomen
  • Elevated liver enzymes in routine checkups
Specialist Focus

Metabolic Evaluation

Comprehensive liver function tests, viral screening, and structured lifestyle-dietary reversal protocols for fatty liver disease.

Consult Dr. Deepak

Specialized Clinical Procedures

Procedures & Diagnostic Care

Advanced endoscopic evaluations and surgical interventions performed with patient-first precision and rigorous safety protocols in Mysuru.

Diagnostic & Therapeutic

Upper GI Endoscopy

A specialized, minimally invasive visual examination of the esophagus, stomach, and duodenum. It allows direct mucosal inspection to accurately diagnose ulcers, acid reflux damage, H. pylori infections, and unexplained upper abdominal pain.

10–15 Mins Day Care Procedure High Comfort & Sedation
Preventive & Screening

Colonoscopy

Comprehensive visual evaluation of the large intestine (colon) and rectum using a flexible high-definition video scope. Crucial for evaluating chronic diarrhea, rectal bleeding, unexplained anemia, and screening for polyps or colonic health.

20–30 Mins Preventive Screening Full Mucosal View
Advanced Non-Invasive

Capsule Endoscopy

A non-invasive diagnostic technique where the patient swallows a small pill-sized video camera that captures thousands of high-definition images as it passes naturally through the small intestine—an area difficult to view with standard endoscopes.

Swallowed Pill Camera Small Intestine Focus Completely Non-Invasive
Minimally Invasive Surgery

Advanced Laparoscopic GI Surgery

Specialist surgical interventions performed through tiny keyhole incisions, including gallbladder removal for gallstones, complex hernia repairs, appendix removal, and GI oncology resections. Ensures reduced postoperative pain and rapid recovery.
